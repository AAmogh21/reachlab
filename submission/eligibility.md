## Published checklist

From [Devpost's What to Submit](https://forgehacks-2026.devpost.com/):

- Title, short problem/solution description, and AI + Education track.
- Public online 2–4-minute video explaining problem and demonstrating operation; target 3 minutes.
- GitHub source with a clear README.
- Written users/problem, technical components, and prospective benefit.
- Screenshots, architecture diagram, or testing deployment link.

Missing video or code is ineligible. Prepare offline launch instructions and a dated attribution/AI-assistance log; demonstrate simulation, training, evaluation, and planning limitations. Required public links must be verified before submission. [Organizer update](https://forgehacks-2026.devpost.com/updates/46776-forgehacks-is-live-track-prompts-and-your-participant-packet): track cannot change after submission.
