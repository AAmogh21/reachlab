PREDICT → EXPERIMENT → EXPLAIN

Three ideas. A thousand experiments.

01

A point has two paths.

A two-link arm can often reach the same target with two elbow configurations. Which one avoids the obstacle?

Engineering idea

Reachable distances lie between |L₁−L₂| and L₁+L₂. The law of cosines gives the elbow angle; its positive and negative branches produce two configurations.

02

The shortest line can fail.

The tip may avoid an obstacle while the arm sweeps through it. Find a route where a direct joint motion is blocked.

Engineering idea

A configuration is a pair of joint angles. A* searches this space. All link segments and swept edges need collision checks; checking only the tip is insufficient.

03

99% is not a guarantee.

Train on 100, then 1,500 examples. Compare missed collisions, not just accuracy. What would happen near a decision boundary?

Engineering idea

kNN predicts from nearby training examples. Collision recall asks how many true collisions were caught. A high overall accuracy can conceal missed collisions.
